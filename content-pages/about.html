About WordRush

WordRush is a fast word game played by tracing adjacent letters on a board. Find a path, submit the word, and keep searching before the round ends.

Play WordRush →

One idea, plenty of ways to play

Every game starts with the same word-tracing idea, but WordRush is not one fixed puzzle. A mode can change the board, clock, goal, minimum word length, or add a special rule that shifts how you search.

That means you can settle into a familiar round, chase a score target, look for longer words, or take on a board with its own twist. See How to Play for the core rules, or compare the current options on Game Modes.

Quick to start, better together

WordRush is playable directly in your browser, whether you want a short solo round or a room with friends. Solo play lets you take on a board on your own; multiplayer puts players on the same board and clock for a shared round.

The goal is simple: make it easy to start a game, then give the same core challenge enough variety to keep the next round interesting.
