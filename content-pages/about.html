About WordRush

WordRush is a fast word game played by tracing adjacent letters on a board. Find a path, submit the word, and keep searching before the round ends.

Play WordRush →

One idea, plenty of ways to play

At the heart of WordRush is a simple challenge: trace words across a board of adjacent letters before time runs out. From there, each game changes the pressure. The board might get bigger, the clock shorter, the minimum word longer, or a special rule might completely change what you look for.

That means you can settle into a familiar round, chase a score target, hunt for longer words, or take on a board with its own twist. See How to Play for the core rules, or compare the current options on Game Modes.

Quick to start, better together

WordRush is playable directly in your browser, whether you want a short solo round or a room with friends. Solo play lets you take on a board on your own; multiplayer puts everyone on the same board and clock so you can compete or play together.

Rounds are quick to start, easy to understand, and varied enough that the next one does not have to feel like the last.
